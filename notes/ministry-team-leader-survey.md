# Ministry Team Leader Survey (Typeform draft)

**Status:** Draft for build in Typeform  
**Audience:** Ministry / volunteer team leaders at Strasburg Baptist Church  
**Goal:** Map how each team is staffed, how they communicate, when they meet, where friction is, and what’s working well  
**Est. completion time:** 5–7 minutes  
**Suggested Typeform title:** Ministry Team Check-In

> No Typeform API access in this workspace — use this doc as a copy-paste build guide in the Typeform editor.  
> Team list below is partial; replace Q3 options when the full ministry list is ready.

---

## Why this survey

Leadership needs a clear picture of each ministry team’s:

1. **Who** is on the team (roster + approximate size)
2. **How** they message and coordinate (tools + primary channel)
3. **If / when** they meet
4. **Pain points** (structured + open)
5. **What’s going well** (so we don’t only collect problems)

Keep tone warm and practical — this is support-seeking, not evaluation.

---

## Typeform settings (recommended)

| Setting | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| Progress bar | On |
| Question numbering | Off (conversational feel) |
| Required questions | All except optional open fields noted below |
| Partial submit | Off |
| Notifications | Email responses to the person collecting results; optional Google Sheets sync |
| Branding | Church name / simple logo; avoid “corporate survey” look |
| Closing | Thank-you ending + optional “we may follow up” note |

**Plan note:** Typeform’s free tier caps questions (~10) and monthly responses. This draft is ~14 blocks (welcome + ending included). Use a paid workspace, or ship the **lean 10-question cut** at the bottom first.

### Welcome Screen

**Title:** Ministry Team Check-In  
**Description:**

Thanks for leading a ministry team at Strasburg Baptist. This short check-in helps us understand who’s serving, how teams communicate, when you meet, and how we can better support you.

It takes about 5–7 minutes. Answer for the team you lead most directly. If you lead more than one team, please submit once per team.

### Q11 — Pain points  
**Type:** Multiple choice — allow multiple answers  
**Required:** Yes  
**Question:** Where does your team feel the most friction right now?  
**Helper:** Select all that apply. It’s okay if things are mostly fine — there’s an option for that.  
**Choices:**

- Hard to get responses or confirmations
- People struggle to show up consistently
- Unclear roles or expectations
- Scheduling conflicts
- Not enough volunteers / people are stretched thin
- Communication is scattered across too many tools
- Hard to onboard new people
- Unclear how this team connects to church leadership
- Need better tools, supplies, or budget clarity
- I don’t have major pain points right now
- Other

## Lean 10-question cut (free-plan / faster)

Use this if you need fewer Typeform questions:

1. Name  
2. Email  
3. Which team  
4. Who’s on the team (long text; include approximate size in helper)  
5. How do you communicate? (multi-select tools)  
6. Primary channel (single select)  
7. How often do you meet? (include “rarely/never”)  
8. When do you usually meet? *(or merge into Q7 helper if needed)*  
9. Biggest friction / pain points (multi-select + Other)  
10. What’s going really well? (long text)

Put co-leader, optional strengths, and follow-up OK into the welcome/ending copy or a later pulse survey.
