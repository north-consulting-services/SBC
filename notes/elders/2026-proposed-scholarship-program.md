## Proposals

1. **For Young Men:** A scholarship for young men going into trades or starting their own business.
2. **For Young Women:** A scholarship for young women wanting to learn crafts or trades that will serve their family someday. Examples of expensive necessary items: mixers, cream separators, sewing machines, etc.
3. **For College Students:** A scholarship for a young person (man or woman) going to college.
   - Steep stipulations: most degrees do not prepare you for the real world or have a benefit.
   - Elders must know what the student’s field of study is and ensure it aligns with the culture we are creating here at SBC.
   - **Handwritten:** field of study

## Requirements

1. Age 16–24
2. Must be a member of SBC
3. Must turn in a written essay requesting the need for the scholarship
4. Must have one or two recommendation letters from family or friends regarding the person’s character
   - Needs Elder discussion to define specific criteria for these letters

## Present Day

1. Would like to be set up by August for presentation for the 2026–2027 school year / work year.
2. Amount of scholarship will vary based on need.
3. Want to provide a way to help young people without them having to take money from organizations that do not Love the Lord.
4. I will commit $15k to start the fund and intend to commit $15k as a base fund annually for the foreseeable future.

## Future

1. Hope is that the fund will grow with other members contributing and eventually past recipients.
2. Hope is that we will see young men start businesses that will hire the next generation.
3. Hope is that this fund will encourage young people that we desire to walk beside them and see them grow in their walk with the Lord.
