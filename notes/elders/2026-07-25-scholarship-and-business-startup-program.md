Through ongoing generous donations, SBC is making some monies available to students, entrepreneurs, and tradesmen on a yearly basis through an application process.

## What the S&BSP will fund

- Students seeking higher education
- Individuals learning a craft or a trade
- Entrepreneurs seeking startup seed monies
- Individuals seeking certifications in various fields

## Evaluation process

- SBC Elders will designate individuals responsible for evaluating S&BSP applications (i.e. **S&BSP Evaluation Board**).
- Applications and evaluations will occur basically **twice a year**:
  - **Primary (fall):** Evaluations and awards in **July** each year for the fall school year → applications due by the **end of June**.
  - **Spring:** Applications accepted and evaluated in the fall → applications in by **November**, evaluations complete by **December**.
- The Evaluation Board will award monies based on **fund availability** and **priority ranking of assessed needs**.
- The Evaluation Board will **interview recent recipients yearly** to assess progress and effectiveness of the program.

## Application criteria

- Must be a **member of SBC**
- Preferred age range **16–24** (other ages will be considered)
- Submit a **written essay** explaining vision, plan, and need for the monies
- Submit **at least two letters of recommendation** pertaining to character and abilities
- Submit a **detailed account of expected costs** and monies requested

**Note:** It is recommended that applicants be enrolled in or have gone through the SBC Discipleship programs (e.g. the Shepherds program and Homemakers(?) program).

## Financial management

- SBC will hold all S&BSP funds in a **designated account**
- Monies for the S&BSP will appear as a **line item in the general SBC budget**
- **Unused funds roll over** into the next year (account can accrue)
- Individuals can donate by **designating** their giving amount to S&BSP
- **Initial deposit for 2026: $15,000**
